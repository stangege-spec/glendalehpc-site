Standards

The HPC Code of Ethics

Every HPC member is held to a shared professional standard. When you work with an HPC coach, you're working with someone who takes their craft — and their community — seriously.

Maintain current certifications in your field, CPR/AED and professional liability insurance — on file with the HPC at all times.

Be respectful of other coaches and clients, honoring zone boundaries so everyone can deliver their absolute best service.

Everything in its place. Wipe down and return equipment after every use. Clean common areas to reflect high standards.

Do right by your clients — be on time, be engaged, and deliver a safe, effective, professional service every session.

Accountability is shared. As a collaborative, what happens to one of us affects us all. Be a good member.
